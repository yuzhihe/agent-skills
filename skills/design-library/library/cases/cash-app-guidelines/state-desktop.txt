					114 text Accessibility
					115 container
						116 text Good accessibility starts with legible text. In digital design, this means ensuring strong contrast between text and background colors. Cash App follows WCAG guidelines, aiming for AAA compliance—the highest standard. This means a contrast ratio of at least 7:1 for normal text to ensure maximum readability. Cash Green is specifically created to work with black text, not green text. Avoid using Cash Green as a critical design element where contrast requirements are not met.
